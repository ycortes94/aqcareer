Achieving upward social mobility can seem like an impossible dream for low-income families. As a first-generation college graduate, I understand the power of higher education and the transformative effect it can have on individuals, families, and their communities at large. By utilizing tools like the Economic Mobility Index and predictive analytics models for decision making, families can make informed choices and increase their chances of success.


Resources of this kind are crucial for low-income families as they navigate the complex world of higher education and career planning. With access to these tools and guidance about possible ways to maximize these resources, families can identify colleges that have a proven record of helping students from similar backgrounds succeed in their chosen careers, ultimately achieving the economic mobility they need to improve their lives.


Moreover, it is important to mention that the shared responsibility of improving access to higher education and achieving social mobility should not solely rest on the shoulders of low-income families alone. The United States needs a more equitable education system that provides all students with the resources they need to succeed.
