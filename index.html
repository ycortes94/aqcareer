about Alina

I'm Alina Quintana, a National Certified Counselor and Certified Career Counselor based in Sacramento, California.

I help organizations create culturally informed career programs and resources that reflect people's identities, lived experiences, and values. My work brings together career development, curriculum design, facilitation, and a commitment to more equitable pathways to mobility.

In 2027, I'll open my practice to a limited number of individuals seeking career guidance that honors identity and culture.

Those I build with

ERICK JOYA
Automotive Industry
Associate to Mid-Level

Alina was a huge help in revising my resume when I was applying to a new position. I ultimately landed the position and wouldn’t have done it without her help. Alina is very patient and all around knowledgeable in many aspects of life. Tech Industry
Associate to Mid-Level

Alina helped me land my first job after college and then transition to a new position that was better suited to my degree where I am now an integral part of the team. Alina is an exceptional career counselor who gave me the tools to become a competitive candidate and the ability to make my experience speak for themselves. She helped me realize that I already have the skills needed to succeed as an Engineer and showed me that I am qualified, and sometimes overqualified, for certain roles and positions when I didn’t believe I was the right candidate. She’s helped me realize my worth as a professional and her guidance assisted me in landing various interviews and at the end, land a great career opportunity in tech. I can say without a doubt that if it wasn’t for Alina’s help, I would not be where I am today.

Education Industry
Entry level and to Specialist

Alina provided me with the guidance and support that I needed during the application period and transition into the position for my current and prior positions. My experience began in 2021 where Alina helped me with my resume when applying for a promotion. She guided me while also providing me the opportunity to learn how to articulate my experiences through writing. I contacted Alina to receive assistance in how I can best assist my assigned high school students with their resumes despite their limited experience and gave me tips that allowed me to feel more comfortable in starting a new role. I felt better equipped for a role that I truly felt under qualified for.

Overall, Alina has helped me become confident in the professional that I am. Even when I felt that I was not competitive compared to other applicants because of my limited experience, Alina provided me with the reassurance and confidence that I needed to make my next step. Alina provides a great balance of guidance and empowerment. She is a great teacher, but also gives you and your voice the power. She challenges you and gives you opportunities to think. Because of her help, I have grown to become extremely confident as a professional as I am better able to articulate my experiences in a professional manner that still reflects who I am.

I full heartedly recommend others to Alina’s career counseling services. As a first-generation Latina professional, I always felt behind as my knowledge and experience were limited, but Alina helped me realize that I have more value and knowledge than I initially thought. I can honestly say that because of my experiences with her, I have become comfortable in seeking opportunities to grow as a professional and help others do the same. I know that if I recommend Alina to anyone, them and their career are in good hands because of the long-lasting impact that her personalized services have.

services

Career Counseling
(Q3 2027 – Limited Clients)

Beginning in Q3 of 2027, I will open my practice to a small number of individual clients seeking career guidance that honors identity, culture, lived experience, and values. This work supports people in making intentional decisions, building agency, and shaping futures that feel aligned and meaningful.

Join the 2027 interest list →

Curriculum Contracts

I partner with organizations to design culturally informed and equity-centered career development curriculum. My approach strengthens community agency, expands access to opportunity, and supports learners in navigating work, advancement, and identity with intention. Curriculum can be adapted for programs, training, public sector initiatives, or community-based organizations.

Workshops

I facilitate workshops on topics related to career development, culture, and agency, with a focus on equity and first-generation experiences. Workshops are designed to create insight, reflection, and practical tools for participants while strengthening community connection and shared learning.

Speaking

I speak on the intersections of career, culture, labor, first-generation identity, and economic mobility. My talks invite audiences to rethink how careers are shaped and how communities build agency through work, lineage, and self-determined futures.
